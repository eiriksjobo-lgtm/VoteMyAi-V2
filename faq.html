Frequently Asked Questions

What is VoteMyAI?
VoteMyAI is a platform where you can submit AI-generated music tracks and let the community rate them. The best-rated tracks rise to the top of the leaderboard.
How do I submit a track?
Click "Submit" in the navigation, log in with your Google account, and paste a link from Suno, Udio, YouTube, or SoundCloud. Used a different AI tool? Just upload your track to YouTube or SoundCloud first, then submit the link.
Which platforms are supported?
We support direct embeds from Suno, Udio, YouTube, and SoundCloud. Since you can upload any audio to YouTube or SoundCloud, tracks made with any AI tool can be submitted.
Do I need to log in to rate tracks?
No! You can rate any track from 1 to 5 stars without logging in. However, logging in lets you submit tracks and leave comments.
Can I rate the same track more than once?
No, each person can rate a track once. We use a combination of browser tokens and server-side checks to keep ratings fair.
Which AI music tools are supported?
Any AI music tool works — Suno, Udio, ElevenLabs, Stable Audio, Boomy, MusicGPT, and more. Just select the tool you used when submitting, or choose "Other" if yours isn't listed.
How does the leaderboard work?
The leaderboard ranks tracks using a Wilson Score algorithm. This means a track with 10 ratings averaging 4.5 stars will rank higher than a track with 1 rating of 5 stars. It rewards both quality and engagement.
What genres can I submit?
We support over 100 genres and subgenres — from Pop and Hip-Hop to Synthwave, Italo Disco, Amapiano, and more. You can also type in a custom genre if yours isn't listed.
Is VoteMyAI free?
Yes! Submitting tracks, rating, and commenting is completely free.
Can I delete my submitted tracks?
Yes. Contact us through the Contact page and we'll remove your track.
Who can I contact for help?
Visit our Contact page to send us a message. We typically respond within 24 hours.
