About VoteMyAI

VoteMyAI was born from a simple idea: AI-generated music is exploding, but there's no good place to discover the best of it.

We built this platform so creators can share their AI tracks and let the community decide what's worth listening to. No algorithms picking winners — just real people rating real music.

How It Works

Submit a track made with any AI music tool — Suno, Udio, ElevenLabs, or anything else. The community listens and rates it from 1 to 5 stars. The best tracks rise to the leaderboard, ranked by a Wilson Score algorithm that rewards both quality and quantity of ratings.

For Creators

Whether you're experimenting with AI music for the first time or you're a seasoned prompt engineer crafting full albums — this is your stage. Get honest feedback, discover what resonates, and see how your tracks stack up.

Independent & Ad-Free*

VoteMyAI is an independent project built in Norway. We're not backed by any AI music company — just passionate about where music is heading. The platform is free to use for everyone.

*We may introduce non-intrusive ads in the future to keep the platform running.
